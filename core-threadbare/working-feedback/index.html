What makes a game good? What makes it great?

A playtest session - pair up with another team, play each other's StoryQuests, and turn what you notice into your next tasks.

60 minutes Feedback session Playtest

This is a recurring feedback session (“Feedback Time”), used in Sessions 11 and 14. You'll pair with another team, playtest, and decide what to improve next.

Meet Playtest Decide
Meet

Come together right now

5 min

Your team will get together with another team to playtest each other's work, then collect feedback to keep polishing your prototype.

  • Your facilitator will use a randomizer to pair up the teams.
  • While that happens, one person per team makes a copy of the Feedback Sheet template and grants edit access to their team.
  • Read the template and complete the section "Before You Meet with Another Team."
Open: Feedback Sheet template ↗

You'll do this formally at least twice at this stage, so make a fresh copy of the template each time. It also helps a lot to playtest with someone "from the outside" when you can.

Playtest

Playtest time!

20 min

Playtest the other team's StoryQuest as thoughtfully and thoroughly as you'd want them to playtest yours.

  • Take notes on whatever you feel as you play - especially when you hit a challenge (did you enjoy it? was it frustrating?).
  • You'll share your notes with the other team next, so don't forget to write them down.

The more people playtest your prototype, the better - so every member of every team should be playing right now. This is about reciprocity.

Decide

Share the results

5 min

Now go through the Share and Talk and Decide What to Do Next sections of the template:

  • Talk first with the other team and take notes on their playtesting experience. Follow the questions in your sheet, and add anything else that came up.
  • Then talk with your own team to decide which tasks you really need to work on. Prioritize, so you make the most of your time.

Use the rest of your session to start on the tasks you just prioritized - the feedback is only useful once it turns into work on the board.
