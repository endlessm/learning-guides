The bones of a game

Every game rests on two “bones”:

  • A story - not every game has one, but when it does it’s easy to spot: a main character, with a beginning, a middle, and an end.
  • A core loop - the engine that keeps a game moving through constant repetition.

A core loop is three beats that repeat: Actions → Reward → Expansion. Every time the loop closes, the stakes get a little higher - and that’s what keeps a player hooked.

Reflect
