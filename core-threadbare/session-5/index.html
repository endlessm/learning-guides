Working with others

5 min

Working in a team is always a challenge in itself - and then there's the actual task to complete. A StoryQuest is a team effort, as you'll see.

  • What is one good thing that happened to you while working in a team?
  • What is one thing you'd have faced differently in a team project?

Video games aren't a solo endeavor (with a few exceptions). On your StoryQuest you'll take on a role - maybe more than one, and you might switch partway through - to bring your magic to the table. Today you'll see how you can work with a partner: literally, and through Backstitch.

Create

Team up

10 min

For the Void Challenge you'll work with a partner. Pairs work best; three is fine if that's how it shakes out. This group is just for this challenge - it doesn't have to become your permanent StoryQuest team.

1

Find a partner and break the ice

Once you're together, each of you write a weird one-line description of yourself and share it. Pick a prompt: If you were a food, what would you be, and why? If you were a sea creature, what would you be, and why?

Describing yourself in a weird way is also an ideation warm-up. One image - say, a jellyfish that glides gracefully through challenges but can sting to defend its project - can surface your strengths and get you ready to roll.

2

Get into the same project

This time, Backstitch is your main tool for working together. Choose one partner's project to be your shared playground for today. That owner shares their Project ID, and the other teammate joins it - so you're both working in the same project. Your projects stay safe on the Platform, so nothing gets lost.

The Void Challenge

35 min

Meet the Void - the "character" at the heart of this challenge. It wanders, chases, eats tilemaps, and ends your run on contact. What that means, and why the player is running, is up to you.

1

Start with a story

Brainstorm what your chase is about - who's running, and why. Land on a sentence or two of narrative context for the chase. (The guide's "What is this chase about" and "Some context for the chase" have prompts to spark ideas.)

2

Build the chase

In your shared project, shape a space with TileMaps, give the Void a path to follow, and place decorative assets as obstacles so escaping feels like a real challenge. The Void guide walks through each part - work from it rather than from here.

3

Work in sub-branches

So you don't overwrite each other, each teammate works in their own sub-branch, then merges the work together. See "Working in a group" in the Backstitch guide.
