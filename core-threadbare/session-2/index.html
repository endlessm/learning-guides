Where ideas come from

How do you come up with new ideas? 5 min

Creating something simple (like a new recipe for a cookie) or something complex (like a space shuttle) starts with an idea - probably a small one.

  • Think of something you created (like a song you wrote) and retrace your steps. What was the spark that lit the fire?
  • Where do your ideas typically come from?

Try this: go back to the beginning of a project and share it. Was there a process that led you to the spark - or did it just arrive?

Create

Blue Sky Brainstorming

When you're facing a big task like building a StoryQuest prototype, you can freeze at the very beginning:

  • What's the story?
  • What's the core loop?
  • Will you be able to handle the tools you'll need to use?

Blue Sky Brainstorming is about being free of any pressure, letting go of the tools, and dreaming up as many big or small ideas as possible.

Blank-page fear is real - and too much freedom is actually counterproductive, so Blue Sky gives you one constraint: don't think about the "how-to" or the right tools yet. Blue sky, no clouds - just let everything go, and write.

Brainstorm #1 - fill it and combine

25 min
1

Fill each category (5 min)

Go category to category - Locations, Characters, Scenes/Scenarios. Use sticky notes to write whatever comes to mind.

2

Review what others wrote (5 min)

Read through everyone's sticky notes across the categories.

3

Combine into one idea

Take one sticky from each category (it could be someone else's!) and write a single sentence that brings it all together - that's a potential StoryQuest idea!

Share the ideas

5 min

Your task

  • 1Post the board in the Feed (just one person needs to do this).
  • 2Share your sentences in the Feed too, so people can leave feedback - or just to show off your cool idea! Ideally, post them as comments on the original board post.

Sharing like this is good practice. StoryQuests are best built in teams, and your team might want to use your idea - or take a really cool part of it - to build the prototype.
